
# HerVoice AI: Early Risk Detection for PCOS & Endometriosis

HerVoice AI is a machine learning–based health analytics project focused on **early risk detection of PCOS and Endometriosis** using patient-related features and predictive modeling.  
The goal of this project is to assist in **early screening and awareness**, not medical diagnosis.


##  Project Motivation
PCOS and Endometriosis are often **underdiagnosed** due to delayed symptom recognition and lack of early screening tools.  
This project explores how **data-driven approaches** can help identify risk patterns early and support preventive healthcare insights.

---

##  Problem Statement
- Delayed diagnosis leads to long-term health complications
- Manual screening is time-consuming and inconsistent
- Need for scalable, data-driven early risk assessment

---
